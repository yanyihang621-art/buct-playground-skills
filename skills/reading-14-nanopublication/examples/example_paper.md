# Oxygen Vacancies and Water Co-feeding in Cu-In2O3 Catalysis (示例，非正式案例)

## 1 Introduction

Oxygen vacancies on Cu-In2O3 increase methanol selectivity from 72% to 91% at 573 K and 3.0 MPa.

## 2 Results

Surface hydroxyl accumulation may induce partial sintering after 200 h. In contrast, carrier gas switching showed no significant difference in CO2 conversion across duplicate runs.
