# Field Protocol: Outdoor Passive-Reference Drift Trial

Table 1 summarizes our 30-run outdoor transition trial conducted at 15-40 C and 45-85% RH without active heating or cooling.
Across all 30 outdoor runs, the passive-reference calibration reduced mean calibration drift from 3.4% to 1.3% under battery power below 0.2 W.
Because trials were conducted at a single coastal test site, multi-season drift across arid climates remains unverified.
