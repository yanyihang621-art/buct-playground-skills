# Cu-In2O3 and Cu-ZnO Catalysts for CO2 Hydrogenation (示例，非正式案例)

## 1 Introduction

Catalytic CO2 hydrogenation to methanol mitigates carbon emissions while storing renewable hydrogen. However, conventional Cu-ZnO catalysts suffer from rapid sintering under high water partial pressure.

## 2 Methods and Conditions

Catalysts were evaluated in a fixed-bed reactor at 573 K, 3.0 MPa, and GHSV = 12000 mL g^-1 h^-1 with H2/CO2 = 3:1 over 100 h on stream.

## 3 Results and Limitations

Table 1 reports steady-state performance after 100 h.

| Catalyst | Temperature (K) | Pressure (MPa) | Methanol Selectivity (%) | CO2 Conversion (%) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Cu-In2O3 | 573 | 3.0 | 91.8 | 14.2 |
| Cu-ZnO | 573 | 3.0 | 72.0 | 11.5 |

Water co-feeding at 10 vol% did not cause irreversible deactivation of Cu-In2O3, whereas Cu-ZnO lost 28% activity. Long-term stability beyond 200 h and regeneration cost were not reported in this study.
